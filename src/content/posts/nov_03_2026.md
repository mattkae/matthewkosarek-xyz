## What have I been up to?
That was a busy start of autumn and end of summer, but I suppose that is what I get for doing
things.

Miracle had a [nice release](https://github.com/miracle-wm-org/miracle-wm/releases/tag/v0.11.0) in
that time, followed by a few patch releases to fix some funky bugs. The main feature in 0.11 is
an overview mode that you can use to switch workspaces and refocus windows. For the next
release, I plan to add support for woblly windows. I've played around with the coding agents a
bunch to "spike" an implementation, but I haven't had enough time to play around with it thus
far to bring it to a point where I am happy with it. There is so much that these AI tools can
do, but I cannot in my right mind recommend that someone use the work produced by them
without validation and heavy refactoring around structure (they often choose ridiculous structures
-- yes even now!).

Speaking of ridiculously vibe-coded software projects, I also made public my fully AI-generated
desktop environment for Linux, aptly titled [Moonswing](https://github.com/miracle-wm-org/moonswing)
(because the AI is often swinging for the moon with many moodswings). I have to say: it does work!
I plan on making a video about it soon. It is a full desktop environment out of the box with support
for a ton of features that you'd expect to "just work" like screensharing, clipboard support, multiple
panels, sound controls, application launching, and so much more that I cannot even begin to describe it.
While it is a fully-featured and somewhat stable desktop, I cannot in my right mind recommend that
you use it to daily-drive your every day life. I hardly ever look at the code and on the off chance
that I do, it is apparent to me that it is absolutely and utterly wacky. The AI has made a nice big
project that is largely illegible to a human being. One good day, I will sit down and parse through
the 150k+ lines that it spit out to see if I can make sense of it and clean it up, but today is certainly
not that day. Use Moonswing if you want, but have low expectations for it. It is entirely exerpimental.

There are a bunch of other thoughts swirling around in my head nowadays, whether that be the economy,
AI, my own personal life, the news, and more. Over the last few months, I've made more of an effort
to stay away from social media and the like, finding that it fills my head with more nonsense than
it is worth. I've had varying success with this, but I think that it is a better thing to keep in mind
than endlessly consuming media. There's something very human about being human that we want to gobble
up information endlessly in order to form some understanding of the world, isn't there? But often the
information that we're currently consuming is endless and depressing and not even useful or relevant
to our own lives. Some AI blog post comes out every other day telling us that the world is ending
or there will be no jobs or X or Y or Z. Yet my experience on the ground is much different, much more
based in reality. I think this is where I'll continue to live until I'm provided with evidence to the
contrary. I will keep up with the developments of the world through my job and through my craft, not
shying away from them of course, but not turning on the firehose of information either.

As always, I hope you have a great autumn and that you see a nice cloud soon (note: a real cloud,
not a server)!
